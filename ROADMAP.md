# Roadmap

BITROT_ is free and will stay free. There's no paid tier and no plan to add one — no locked layers, no feature gating, no account. A "pay what you want" support link is planned for later; it will get you nothing extra, it'll just say thanks.

This page is a running list of what's planned, being considered, or deliberately out of scope. Nothing here has a date — it gets built when it gets built.

## Planned

- **Case-file export** — a before/after image with the recipe and a hex dump of the corrupted byte offsets, for the forensics angle. The biggest planned feature.
- **Smoother dragging on heavy stacks** — a fast, low-res preview while a number is being dragged, so byte corruption and other slow layers don't freeze the canvas until you let go.
- **Drag-to-reorder layers** — alongside the existing ▲ / ▼ buttons.
- **Closer match between preview and export** — exports are re-rendered at the export width, so very fine detail can currently differ slightly from what's on screen.

## Under consideration

- More layers: halftone, gradient maps, sort-along-a-path.
- Paint-on masking, so a glitch only applies to part of the image.
- A short guided tour on first visit.
- Keyboard polish: Home/End on the number dials, and reduced-motion handling for auto-scrolling.

## Won't do

- **No paid tier, ever.** Every effect, export format and social crop stays free for everyone.
- **No account, no image upload, no tracking.** Everything still runs in your browser.

## Have an idea?

Open an [issue](https://github.com/untruesudo/bitrot-canvas/issues) — feature requests and bug reports are both welcome.
